## About

I am a computer scientist working at the intersection of **IoT, environmental sensing, and AI**. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UCLA, and my research focuses on mobile wireless networks and spatio-temporal big data analysis.

My best-known work is the **Micro PM2.5 Sensor System (AirBox / LASS)** — an open, participatory air-quality sensing network with more than **20,000 sensors deployed in 59 countries**, spanning low-cost hardware, open-source firmware, open data services, and real-time analytics for smart cities.

Today my work also covers **AI adoption and AI literacy**: leading Taiwan AI Academy's AI capability certification and government AI adoption programs, and bringing generative AI into academic administrative services at Academia Sinica.
